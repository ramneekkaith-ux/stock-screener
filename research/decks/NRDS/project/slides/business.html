Business overview

A personal-finance marketplace funded by referral fees

Consumers and small businesses compare financial products; providers pay per lead or per approval

1. Business overview

  • Based in San Francisco, with about 650 employees. Co-founder Tim Chen is CEO.
  • Revenue is reported for two user groups from 2026: Consumer ($214.5m in Q3, up 12%) and SMB ($25.1m, up 6%).
  • In Q3, the main drivers were student loans (+$25.0m), personal loans (+$13.3m) and deposits (+$8.6m), offset by auto insurance (–$17.4m).

2. Segments

Consumer

Credit cards, loans, banking, insurance and investing referrals. 90% of Q3 2026 revenue.

SMB

Small-business loans and financial products. 10% of Q3 2026 revenue.
