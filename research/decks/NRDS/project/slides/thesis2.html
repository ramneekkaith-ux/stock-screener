2. Buybacks are shrinking the share count fast

$99m repurchased in nine months, about a fifth of the current market value

The argument

  • Buybacks in the first nine months of 2026 were $99.3m, five times the prior-year period ($19.5m).
  • At today's price, continued buybacks at that pace would retire a large share of the company each year.
  • Cash fell to $73.5m, so future buybacks need to come from operating cash flow, which is still positive.

What would prove us wrong

Cash flow falls far enough that buybacks stop, or the company does a large acquisition instead.
