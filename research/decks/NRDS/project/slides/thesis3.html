3. The growth engines outside auto insurance are working

Revenue grew 11% in Q3 despite a $17m auto-insurance hole

The argument

  • Lending and banking more than offset auto insurance in Q3: student loans +$25.0m, personal loans +$13.3m, deposits +$8.6m.
  • Q3 revenue beat consensus. The miss was on profit, because marketing spend rose faster than revenue.
  • If auto-insurance partnerships are rebuilt in 2027, operating leverage could return quickly. This is our recovery case ($16.55).

What would prove us wrong

Lending demand weakens with the credit cycle at the same time as the auto-insurance gap persists.
