1. The valuation already prices in a lot of damage

Even our AI-disruption scenario is worth more than the share price

The argument

  • EV is about 3.4x LTM EBITDA and 3x LTM free cash flow, against a peer median EV/EBITDA of about 9x.
  • Our bear case assumes flat revenue in 2027, then a 1–2% decline a year with margins stuck at 6%. It still gives $9.65 a share.
  • Net cash and positive cash flow mean the company does not need outside capital to get through the auto-insurance gap.

What would prove us wrong

Revenue keeps falling well beyond Q4 2026's 13% decline, or margins go negative again as marketing costs rise.
