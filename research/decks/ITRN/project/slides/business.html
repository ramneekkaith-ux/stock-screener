Business overview

Telematics subscriptions across Israel and Latin America

Stolen-vehicle recovery, fleet management and connected-car services, sold through insurers, car makers and directly

1. Business overview

  • Listed on Nasdaq; headquartered in Azour, Israel.
  • Revenue in Q3 2025 by region: Israel 55%, Brazil 23%, rest of world 22%.
  • Subscriber base: 2,711,000 at June 2026, up 163,000 year on year.
  • Led by co-CEOs from the founding Sheratzky family. Insiders hold about 20% of the shares.

2. Segments

Subscription fees (76% of Q2 2026 revenue)

Monthly fees for stolen-vehicle recovery, tracking, fleet management and usage-based insurance data. $80m in Q2 2026, up 25%.

Products (24%)

Telematics devices sold to OEMs, dealers and fleets. $25.0m in Q2 2026, up 8%. Lower margin, but each unit sold brings a subscriber.
