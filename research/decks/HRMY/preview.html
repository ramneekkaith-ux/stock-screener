Harmony Biosciences: the market is pricing WAKIX as a melting ice cube, and the pipeline comes free

A ~$1bn drug with cover until 2030 and $775m of cash, valued at about 5x EBITDA. Lifecycle products are the swing factor

1. Narrative

  • WAKIX (pitolisant) is on track for more than $1bn of net revenue in 2026. Q3 2026 sales were $269m, and full-year guidance of $1.0–1.04bn was reiterated.
  • Generic entry is settled with 6 of 7 ANDA filers at no earlier than March 2030 if pediatric exclusivity is granted (September 2029 without it). That leaves roughly three more years of high-margin cash flow.
  • The company generated $323m of free cash flow over the last 12 months and holds $775m of cash against $164m of debt. Its enterprise value is only about 1.4x revenue.
  • Our WAKIX-only run-off DCF ($31) is close to the share price, so the market gives almost no value to pitolisant GR (PDUFA 1 April 2027), pitolisant HD (2028) or the BP-205 orexin agonist.
  • A 60/40 weighting of run-off and lifecycle-success scenarios gives a $39 target (+16%). Takeda's newly approved Orzeyful is the key competitive risk.

Business overview

A one-product rare-CNS company building its next franchise

WAKIX for narcolepsy today; next-generation pitolisant and an orexin agonist in development

1. Business overview

  • US commercial-stage pharma company based in Plymouth Meeting, Pennsylvania. It was founded by Paragon Biosciences.
  • WAKIX was approved by the FDA in August 2019 for narcolepsy. It is the only non-scheduled (non-controlled) treatment for excessive daytime sleepiness or cataplexy in narcolepsy.
  • COO Peter Anastasiou succeeds CEO Jeffrey Dayno, MD, on 1 January 2027.
  • Institutions own most of the free float. Insiders hold about 2%.

2. Segments

WAKIX (essentially all revenue)

Net revenue of $868.5m in 2025 and guided to $1.0–1.04bn in 2026. About 400 net patient adds a quarter.

Pipeline

Pitolisant GR (NDA filed, PDUFA 1 Apr 2027), pitolisant HD (Phase 3 data 2027, target PDUFA 2028), BP-205 orexin-2 agonist (Phase 1b data Q1 2027), plus EPX-100 in epilepsy.

Industry overview

Narcolepsy treatment is entering a new drug class

Orexin agonists target the cause of narcolepsy type 1; WAKIX's niche is its non-controlled status

1. Market

  • Narcolepsy type 1 affects about 1 in 2,000 US adults and is caused by the loss of orexin-producing neurons.
  • Treatment mixes wake-promoting drugs, oxybates (Jazz's Xywav, Avadel's Lumryz) and stimulants. Many patients combine therapies.
  • WAKIX's advantage is that it is not a controlled substance, which makes prescribing and refills simpler.

2. Tailwinds

▲ Orexin science validated

Takeda's Orzeyful approval (5 Aug 2026) validates the class Harmony is entering with BP-205.

▲ Steady patient growth

About 400 net patient adds a quarter for WAKIX through 2026.

3. Headwinds

▼ New competition

Orzeyful treats narcolepsy type 1 as a whole, which could take share from symptomatic drugs once DEA scheduling is complete.

▼ Generics

Settled generic entry from March 2030 (or September 2029 without pediatric exclusivity).

Competitive analysis

Cheap against specialty-pharma peers, for a visible reason

High returns today; the debate is how much survives 2030

1. Positioning: ROE vs revenue

  • ROE was 18% in 2025 even with $775m of cash on the balance sheet. Few peers combine this growth with this margin.
  • Peers trade at about 9x EBITDA. Harmony trades at about 5x, because investors look past the patent cliff.

2. Porter’s five forces

Rivalry

Oxybates (Jazz, Avadel), modafinil/armodafinil generics, stimulants and now orexin agonists (Takeda).

Substitutes

Generic wake-promoters are cheap. WAKIX wins on being non-scheduled and well tolerated.

New entrants

High barriers (clinical trials, rare-disease salesforce) until patents expire.

Suppliers

Pitolisant is licensed from Bioprojet. The product depends on that licence.

Buyers

US payers. Rare-disease pricing has held up, but rebates can rise as competition grows.

Investment thesis 1

1. Cash flow before 2030 covers most of the enterprise value

The patent cliff is real, but it is three-plus years away and already priced

The argument

  • WAKIX is guided to $1.0–1.04bn in 2026. With almost no capex, most operating profit converts to cash.
  • In our run-off case, cumulative free cash flow from 2026 to 2029 alone is more than $1bn, against an EV of about $1.37bn.
  • Settlements with 6 of 7 generic filers fix the earliest entry date, which reduces the risk of an early surprise.

What would prove us wrong

The remaining patent case goes against Harmony and allows earlier generic entry, or Orzeyful cuts WAKIX growth sharply before 2030.

Investment thesis 2

2. Lifecycle products could keep the franchise alive past 2030

Pitolisant GR and HD carry patents filed to 2044

The argument

  • Pitolisant GR (gastro-resistant) needs no dose titration. Its NDA is under FDA review, with a PDUFA date of 1 April 2027.
  • Pitolisant HD targets new indications. Phase 3 data are due in 2027, with a target PDUFA in 2028.
  • If even part of the WAKIX base moves to GR/HD before 2030, the revenue cliff becomes a slope. Our lifecycle scenario is worth $52 a share.

What would prove us wrong

Payers and doctors treat GR as a minor reformulation and switch patients to generic pitolisant in 2030.

Investment thesis 3

3. BP-205 is a free option on the orexin class

Takeda's approval shows the class works; Harmony is aiming for best-in-class

The argument

  • Multiple-ascending-dose data for BP-205 supported once-daily dosing, with no liver, visual or significant cardiovascular events reported.
  • Phase 1b data in sleep-deprived volunteers are due in Q1 2027, and Phase 2 studies across several CNS indications are planned by mid-2027.
  • Neither of our DCF scenarios gives any value to BP-205.

What would prove us wrong

Clinical failure or safety signals. Orexin agonists have had liver and visual side effects in earlier programmes.

Upcoming readouts

  • Q1 2027: BP-205 Phase 1b (sleep-deprived healthy volunteers).
  • 1 Apr 2027: pitolisant GR PDUFA date.
  • 2027: pitolisant HD Phase 3 topline; EPX-100 Phase 3 readouts.
