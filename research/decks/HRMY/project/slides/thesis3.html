Investment thesis 3

3. BP-205 is a free option on the orexin class

Takeda's approval shows the class works; Harmony is aiming for best-in-class

The argument

  • Multiple-ascending-dose data for BP-205 supported once-daily dosing, with no liver, visual or significant cardiovascular events reported.
  • Phase 1b data in sleep-deprived volunteers are due in Q1 2027, and Phase 2 studies across several CNS indications are planned by mid-2027.
  • Neither of our DCF scenarios gives any value to BP-205.

What would prove us wrong

Clinical failure or safety signals. Orexin agonists have had liver and visual side effects in earlier programmes.
