Executive summary

Harmony Biosciences: the market is pricing WAKIX as a melting ice cube, and the pipeline comes free

A ~$1bn drug with cover until 2030 and $775m of cash, valued at about 5x EBITDA. Lifecycle products are the swing factor

1. Narrative

  • WAKIX (pitolisant) is on track for more than $1bn of net revenue in 2026. Q3 2026 sales were $269m, and full-year guidance of $1.0–1.04bn was reiterated.
  • Generic entry is settled with 6 of 7 ANDA filers at no earlier than March 2030 if pediatric exclusivity is granted (September 2029 without it). That leaves roughly three more years of high-margin cash flow.
  • The company generated $323m of free cash flow over the last 12 months and holds $775m of cash against $164m of debt. Its enterprise value is only about 1.4x revenue.
  • Our WAKIX-only run-off DCF ($31) is close to the share price, so the market gives almost no value to pitolisant GR (PDUFA 1 April 2027), pitolisant HD (2028) or the BP-205 orexin agonist.
  • A 60/40 weighting of run-off and lifecycle-success scenarios gives a $39 target (+16%). Takeda's newly approved Orzeyful is the key competitive risk.
