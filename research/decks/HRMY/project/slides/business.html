Business overview

A one-product rare-CNS company building its next franchise

WAKIX for narcolepsy today; next-generation pitolisant and an orexin agonist in development

1. Business overview

  • US commercial-stage pharma company based in Plymouth Meeting, Pennsylvania. It was founded by Paragon Biosciences.
  • WAKIX was approved by the FDA in August 2019 for narcolepsy. It is the only non-scheduled (non-controlled) treatment for excessive daytime sleepiness or cataplexy in narcolepsy.
  • COO Peter Anastasiou succeeds CEO Jeffrey Dayno, MD, on 1 January 2027.
  • Institutions own most of the free float. Insiders hold about 2%.

2. Segments

WAKIX (essentially all revenue)

Net revenue of $868.5m in 2025 and guided to $1.0–1.04bn in 2026. About 400 net patient adds a quarter.

Pipeline

Pitolisant GR (NDA filed, PDUFA 1 Apr 2027), pitolisant HD (Phase 3 data 2027, target PDUFA 2028), BP-205 orexin-2 agonist (Phase 1b data Q1 2027), plus EPX-100 in epilepsy.
