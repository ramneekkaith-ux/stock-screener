Business overview

Local reviews and advertising, adding AI services and data licensing

About 509,000 paying advertising locations, plus a fast-growing 'other revenue' line

1. Business overview

  • Headquartered in San Francisco and led by co-founder and CEO Jeremy Stoppelman.
  • Advertising is sold mainly to Services businesses (home and local services) and to Restaurants, Retail & Other (RR&O).
  • Yelp acquired Hatch (AI customer-communication software for service pros) in 2026: $264m of acquisition-related cash outflow year to date.

2. Segments

Advertising (91% of Q3 revenue)

Services $238m (–2%), RR&O $99m (–13%). Average cost per click +2%, ad clicks –8%.

Other revenue (9%)

$34m, up 82%: Hatch (~$37m annual run rate), data licensing with OpenAI and Meta, and food ordering. Target: $250m run rate by end-2028.
