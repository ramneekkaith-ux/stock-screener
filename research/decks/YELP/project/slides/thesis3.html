3. Capital return will restart in 2027

~$200m bought back in 2026 to date; $339m of authorisation left

The argument

  • Yelp repurchased about $200m of stock in 2026 to date, about 18% of today's market value.
  • Buybacks are paused only to repay the $75m revolver drawn for Hatch, and are expected to resume in 2027.
  • At about 3x EBITDA, buybacks are among the best uses of cash available to management.

What would prove us wrong

Further large acquisitions divert cash from buybacks.
