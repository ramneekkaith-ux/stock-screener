Business overview

Flat revenue, but margins doubled since 2022 and free cash flow is strong

4. Key financials

  • Revenue rose from $1.19bn (2022) to $1.47bn (2025). The EBIT margin improved from 5% to 13% as stock-based compensation fell.
  • 2026 guidance: revenue $1.455–1.460bn and adjusted EBITDA $320–325m. SBC is ~7% of revenue and D&A ~5%.
  • Buybacks are paused to repay a $75m revolver drawing (used for Hatch). They are expected to resume in 2027.
