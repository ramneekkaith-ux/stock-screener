Executive summary

Yelp: a cash machine priced for terminal decline

Advertising is shrinking, but AI products and data licensing are growing fast, and the stock trades at ~3x EBITDA

1. Narrative

  • Yelp connects consumers with local businesses and earns most of its revenue from advertising by restaurants, retailers and service businesses.
  • Q3 2026 revenue fell 1% to $371m: advertising was down 6% as ad clicks fell 8%. Other revenue (Hatch, data licensing, food ordering) rose 82%.
  • Profitability is intact: Q3 adjusted EBITDA was $92m (25% margin) and free cash flow $85m. Yelp bought back ~$200m of stock in 2026 to date.
  • At $20.34 the shares trade at about 3x EV/EBITDA, roughly 5x EBIT and 9x earnings, with net cash. That valuation implies a long, steep decline.
  • Even our structural-decline case (revenue –4% a year) is worth $25. A 50/50 blend with stabilisation gives a $33 target, but the debate is real.
