New entrants

The barrier is 20 years of reviews and a trusted brand. That data is hard to rebuild.

Suppliers

Traffic from Google and Apple; data partners (OpenAI, Meta) are also customers.

Buyers

Hundreds of thousands of small advertisers, each with little power, but quick to cut budgets.
