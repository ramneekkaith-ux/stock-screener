Investment thesis 1

1. The valuation assumes a decline worse than our bear case

Yelp generates ~$260m of free cash flow on an EV of ~$810m

The argument

  • EV/EBITDA is about 3.3x and P/E about 9x, against peer medians of about 9x and 17x.
  • Our structural-decline case assumes revenue falls 4% a year for five years and margins compress to 8%. It still gives $25 a share.
  • Net cash and ~$100m of quarterly operating cash flow give the company room to fund the pivot.

What would prove us wrong

Revenue falls much faster than 4% a year as AI answers take over local discovery.
