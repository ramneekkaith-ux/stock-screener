Industry overview

Local search is being rewired by AI

Fewer clicks reach publishers, but local data and reviews become more valuable to AI platforms

1. Market

  • Local advertising follows small-business health. Yelp says restaurants, retail and home services are under pressure.
  • Industry studies suggest AI Overviews now appear in about 68% of local searches, which cuts click-throughs to sites like Yelp.
  • Yelp's content (reviews, photos, business data) is now licensed to AI companies, a new revenue stream.

2. Tailwinds

▲ Data licensing

Agreements with OpenAI and Meta drove the 82% rise in other revenue. Reservations and Waitlist are integrated into ChatGPT.

▲ AI products

Yelp Host (2.5m annual run-rate calls, up 15x) and Smart Lead Reply (launching Q4 2026).

3. Headwinds

▼ Click erosion

Ad clicks fell 8% in Q3 2026, after a 5% fall in Q2.

▼ SMB weakness

Services advertising declined for the first time in at least nine quarters.

Source: Yelp Q3 2026 letter; PPC Land (Nov 2026); BriefGlance (Nov 2026); MarketBeat call summary (8 Nov 2026)
