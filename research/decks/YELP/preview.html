Executive summary

Yelp: a cash machine priced for terminal decline

Advertising is shrinking, but AI products and data licensing are growing fast, and the stock trades at ~3x EBITDA

1. Narrative

  • Yelp connects consumers with local businesses and earns most of its revenue from advertising by restaurants, retailers and service businesses.
  • Q3 2026 revenue fell 1% to $371m: advertising was down 6% as ad clicks fell 8%. Other revenue (Hatch, data licensing, food ordering) rose 82%.
  • Profitability is intact: Q3 adjusted EBITDA was $92m (25% margin) and free cash flow $85m. Yelp bought back ~$200m of stock in 2026 to date.
  • At $20.34 the shares trade at about 3x EV/EBITDA, roughly 5x EBIT and 9x earnings, with net cash. That valuation implies a long, steep decline.
  • Even our structural-decline case (revenue –4% a year) is worth $25. A 50/50 blend with stabilisation gives a $33 target, but the debate is real.

Industry overview

Local search is being rewired by AI

Fewer clicks reach publishers, but local data and reviews become more valuable to AI platforms

1. Market

  • Local advertising follows small-business health. Yelp says restaurants, retail and home services are under pressure.
  • Industry studies suggest AI Overviews now appear in about 68% of local searches, which cuts click-throughs to sites like Yelp.
  • Yelp's content (reviews, photos, business data) is now licensed to AI companies, a new revenue stream.

2. Tailwinds

▲ Data licensing

Agreements with OpenAI and Meta drove the 82% rise in other revenue. Reservations and Waitlist are integrated into ChatGPT.

▲ AI products

Yelp Host (2.5m annual run-rate calls, up 15x) and Smart Lead Reply (launching Q4 2026).

3. Headwinds

▼ Click erosion

Ad clicks fell 8% in Q3 2026, after a 5% fall in Q2.

▼ SMB weakness

Services advertising declined for the first time in at least nine quarters.

Competitive analysis

The cheapest name among online local and vertical marketplaces

Higher returns on equity than most peers, at a fraction of the multiple

1. Positioning: ROE vs revenue

  • ROE rose to about 20% in 2025 as margins expanded and the share count fell.
  • Google is the dominant competitor for local intent; Angi and Thumbtack compete in home services.

2. Porter’s five forces

Rivalry

Google Maps/Search, Angi, Thumbtack, TripAdvisor, food-delivery apps for restaurants.

Substitutes

AI assistants that answer 'best plumber near me' directly.

New entrants

The barrier is 20 years of reviews and a trusted brand. That data is hard to rebuild.

Suppliers

Traffic from Google and Apple; data partners (OpenAI, Meta) are also customers.

Buyers

Hundreds of thousands of small advertisers, each with little power, but quick to cut budgets.

Investment thesis 1

1. The valuation assumes a decline worse than our bear case

Yelp generates ~$260m of free cash flow on an EV of ~$810m

The argument

  • EV/EBITDA is about 3.3x and P/E about 9x, against peer medians of about 9x and 17x.
  • Our structural-decline case assumes revenue falls 4% a year for five years and margins compress to 8%. It still gives $25 a share.
  • Net cash and ~$100m of quarterly operating cash flow give the company room to fund the pivot.

What would prove us wrong

Revenue falls much faster than 4% a year as AI answers take over local discovery.
